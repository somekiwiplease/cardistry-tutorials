# Cardistry library — move list by level, draft 2

From opening a deck to advanced moves. No magic.

**Where this comes from**
- Move names, creators, difficulty labels and tutorial links: the r/cardistry Cardistry Hub doc (last updated 2021).
- Grade bands: modelled on SQUAREUP's Level 0–9+ scale and the levels shown on Cardistry Base.
- The order inside each grade is my own judgement from those sources. It has not been reviewed by an experienced cardist, and it should be before anyone else relies on it.

**How this is meant to be used**
- This is a library, not a course. Anyone can open any move at any time and click through to the tutorial on YouTube.
- Levels are labels for sorting and filtering. Nothing is locked, required or tracked.
- The order within a level is a suggestion for people who want one.
- Paid moves link to the creator's shop. Never to re-uploads.

Legend: `free` = free tutorial by or with the creator · `preview` = performance only, learn from slow-motion · `paid` = sold by the creator · `link needed` = no working tutorial link found yet

## Level 0 — Before you begin
No moves yet. Short reads and one video each.

| # | Lesson | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Pick a deck | Standard Bicycle, paper, poker size. Cheap, because early decks wear out fast. Avoid plastic and bridge size. `link needed` |
| 0.2 | Open and break in a deck | Riffle, faro, spring and spreads in both directions so the deck stays flat. `link needed` (doc cites Lotus in Hand, 52Kards, Rise Magic) |
| 0.3 | Look after your cards | Clean dry hands, keep away from humidity, back in the box. `link needed` |
| 0.4 | Set up to practise | Over a bed, slow before fast, fewer cards when a grip hurts, short breaks. |
| 0.5 | Left or right hand | Most tutorials hold the deck in the left hand. Mirror the video if you hold it in the right. |
| 0.6 | Basic grips | [Lotus in Hand](https://youtu.be/bt0RumRuwGQ) · [School of Cardistry](https://youtu.be/e8PAMLgLcfY) · [Fontaine](https://youtu.be/ocVFnpsNWpI) |
